# Elayo Family brand assets

Source: https://elayofamily.vercel.app/web_index.html (retrieved 10 October 2026).

The dashboard uses the source site's brand name, Caisse d’entraide Elayo Family, and its Poppins font. Its palette is blue `#0057D9`, dark blue `#0046AE`, light blue `#2E72E0`, gold `#F5B400`, ink `#0B2545`, sky `#EAF1FE`, cloud `#FAFBFE`, and border `#E4ECF7`.

Downloaded assets in `public/images/elayo/`:

- `logo.png`: `assets/logog.png`, full-color logo.
- `logo-white.png`: `assets/white_logo.png`, white logo for the savings cards.
- `favicon.png`: `assets/fav.png`, the original brand symbol; also converted into the application's ICO favicon.
- `community.jpeg`: `assets/pik1.jpeg`, used in the sidebar community card.
- `cooperative.jpeg`: `assets/pik2.jpeg`, used in social-fund introductions.

The source site's “Save together. Grow together.” tagline appears with its community photo. Member identities and financial figures remain the existing illustrative records.
